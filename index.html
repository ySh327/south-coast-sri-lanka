02 — GETTING THERE

Kandy to the south coast

Kandy sits on the main road down to Colombo, so getting south means passing through Colombo one way or another — by car it's a single continuous drive, by bus it's two connecting routes from the same stand. Pick a beach town below and get live directions, or read the three practical ways to make the trip.

Get directions

Opens Google Maps with driving directions from Kandy to your chosen beach town. Switch travel mode inside Maps once it opens.

Open directions in Google Maps

Road distance is roughly 150–225 km depending on the town — 3.5–4.5 hours by car via the Southern Expressway, longer by bus or train.

Bus (two routes, one bus stand) CHEAPEST

Route 01 from Kandy Good Shed Bus Station to Colombo (Bastian Mawatha / Pettah), then Route 02 onward to Galle/Matara, or Route 32 onward to Tangalle/Dikwella — same bus stand in Colombo, no need to cross town. Budget roughly 7–8 hours total and expect standing room in high season, so arrive early for a seat.

Private car or taxi FASTEST

Book a car via PickMe, Uber, or a hotel-arranged driver. Around 3.5–4.5 hours direct via the Southern Expressway, whichever beach town you're headed to. No changes, easiest with luggage, and the only option that lets you stop along the way.

Train + connection CHANGE AT COLOMBO

Train from Kandy to Colombo Fort (about 2.5 hrs, every couple of hours), then an onward train or bus down the coast to Galle or Matara (roughly 3–5 hrs). More comfortable seating than the direct bus, but this is the one option that requires a change and a wait in Colombo — see the timetable below.

TRAIN TIMETABLE

Kandy → Colombo → Galle / Matara, by rail

There's no through train — this is the one option that means changing at Colombo. If you'd rather not change, take the direct bus above instead. Times below are Sri Lanka Railways' standard schedule.

Check before you travel: the Kandy–Colombo hill-country line has been suspended since Cyclone Ditwah damaged the track between Rambukkana and Peradeniya (late 2025) — repairs are targeted for later in 2026 but weren't finished as of the most recent checks. Until it reopens, treat Leg 1 below as reference only and use the bus or a car/taxi to Colombo instead. The Colombo–Galle–Matara coast line (Leg 2) is running normally. Confirm current status at railway.gov.lk or with your hotel before relying on the train.

Almost all seats are unreserved 2nd/3rd class — buy on the day, no need to pre-book. One daily train has a 1st class reserved car.

Two workable same-day connections

  • Early: Kandy 06:15 → Colombo Fort 08:47 → change → Colombo Fort 10:30 → Galle 12:49 / Matara 14:21. Total door to door: ~8 hours including the change.
  • Midday: Kandy 10:40 → Colombo Fort 14:04 → change → Colombo Fort 14:40 → Galle 16:57 / Matara 18:15. Tightest comfortable connection with about 35 minutes in Colombo.

On the return journey, Matara/Galle trains run back to Colombo Fort from as early as 03:15, with Kandy-bound trains from Colombo Fort departing as late as 20:30 (Night Mail) — so a same-day round trip is possible if you leave the coast early.

ALONG THE COAST

Moving between the beaches themselves

Once you're on the coast, every beach in this guide sits on (or just off) one road — the A2, Galle Road. Local buses run the whole line every 15–20 minutes, no booking or timetable needed: just wait at any stop and flag one down.

How it works: buses signed for Matara, Tangalle or Kataragama run the length of this route and will drop you at any beach along it — tell the conductor your stop as you board. Fares are paid on board in cash and are only a few hundred rupees even for a long hop. Unawatuna and Hiriketiya sit just off the main road, so get off at the nearest junction and take a short tuk-tuk the rest of the way.
